Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 May 2026  |  Version 1.0

This Privacy Policy explains how Josea Outsourcing Ltd collects, uses and protects information in connection with the POS V3 Mauritius software.

1. Who We Are

Josea Outsourcing Ltd ("we", "us") is the developer and licensor of POS V3 Mauritius. We are registered in the Republic of Mauritius.

2. Data Collected by the Software

POS V3 is an on-premises application. All business data you enter (products, sales, purchases, VAT records, staff accounts) is stored exclusively in the local SQLite database on your hardware. We do not have access to your business data unless you explicitly share it with us for support purposes.

The following information is collected or transmitted by the Software:

DataPurposeWhere stored
Hardware fingerprint (hashed CPU/disk/MAC) Licence binding & activation Embedded in licence key; transmitted to licence server on activation
Company ID & licence ID Licence validation heartbeat Transmitted to licence server at periodic intervals
Audit log entries (user actions) Compliance & security audit trail Local database only
User account credentials (hashed passwords) Authentication Local database only — passwords are one-way hashed
Automated email backup attachments Off-site backup Sent to email addresses configured by your administrator; encrypted at rest

3. Legal Basis for Processing

We process the limited data described above on the basis of:

  • Contractual necessity — to provide and enforce the software licence;
  • Legitimate interests — to protect our intellectual property and prevent unauthorised use;
  • Legal obligation — to comply with applicable Mauritian law.

4. Data Retention

We retain licence activation records for the duration of your licence plus two (2) years thereafter for auditing purposes. Business data stored locally is under your control and subject to your own retention policies.

5. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical measures to protect data transmitted to our licence server, including HTTPS transport encryption. Database backup files produced by the Software are encrypted using AES-256 (Fernet) with a key derived from your licence. You are responsible for securing access to the application server and backup files on your premises.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your data with third parties except:

  • Where required by law or a competent authority in Mauritius;
  • To service providers acting on our behalf under confidentiality obligations (e.g. hosting for the licence activation server).

7. Your Rights (Data Protection Act 2017 — Mauritius)

Under the Mauritius Data Protection Act 2017 and, where applicable, the GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access personal data we hold about you;
  • Request correction of inaccurate data;
  • Request erasure of data (subject to our legitimate interests and legal obligations);
  • Object to or restrict processing;
  • Lodge a complaint with the Data Protection Office of Mauritius.

To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@josea.mu.

8. Cookies & Browser Storage

The Software uses a single session cookie to maintain your login state within the application. No tracking cookies or third-party analytics are used.

9. Children's Privacy

The Software is intended for use by business professionals only and is not directed at persons under the age of 18.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The current version will always be accessible from the login screen and the application footer. Continued use of the Software after a material change constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
